🧫 Welcome to Petri Evolution!

A living ecosystem in a petri dish. You don't steer the cells — you shape their world and watch evolution do the rest.

🌱 Life

Animals hunt for Food, breathe O₂ and leave behind Waste and CO₂.

Plants clean up after them and spread by Seeds: a seed flies a short way and sprouts where it is mild and there is room, otherwise it is lost.

Every new generation can mutate, so the best-suited cells slowly take over.

♻️ The loop

Animals
Food → Waste
O₂ → CO₂

Plants
Waste → Food
CO₂ → O₂

If one link breaks, the dish slowly starves. A dead cell leaves nothing behind — its energy is simply gone.

⚠️ What can kill your cells

  • Heat Cold Comfort zone is 5–30 °C. Outside it cells burn energy faster (up to 2× at 50 °C or −10 °C) and may die of exhaustion.
  • Overcrowding Animals with more than 3 neighbours squeezed right next to them die, so they try to wander away from crowds.
  • Disease Infected cells cannot reproduce. Survivors can become immune and often pass it on.
  • Asteroids The radar in the top-right shows the next asteroid and its hit chance. A hit kills 10–90 % of all cells, heats the impact spot (+50 °C for a minute) and leaves a crater that fades over 10 minutes.
  • Mass extinction If the population halves within 15 seconds, a red banner shows how big the loss is.

🌗 Day & climate

A full day-and-night cycle lasts about a minute. At night the dish gets darker and colder, and well-fed animals hibernate.

The Climate button paints the temperature on the dish: red is hot, blue is cold. It starts hidden.

🎮 Your tools

  • Spawn Cells adds 10 animals and 10 plants in the middle, next to the resources.
  • Food Management adds or removes resources to rescue or balance the dish.
  • Mutation Rate and Disease Chance set how fast traits change and how often disease strikes.
  • Speed & Pause: 1×, 2× or 5×.
  • Camera: scroll to zoom, drag to move, Fit shows the whole dish.
  • 📖 History Book records the periods of life. A mass extinction that really changes life ends one period and starts the next.

Watch Species Analytics, the Deaths table and the graphs to see who is winning.
